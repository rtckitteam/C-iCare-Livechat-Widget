Anonymous Visitor

The widget is installed with nothing but data-widget-key. The visitor fills in a short pre-chat form before the conversation starts.

This box is a testing aid only. On your own site you write the key straight into the data-widget-key attribute, exactly as in the snippet below.

The snippet you copy into your page

<!-- put this inside <head> -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://cdn.example.com/widget/widget.css">

<!-- put this just before </body> -->
<div id="livechat-app"
     class="livechat-widget"
     data-widget-key="YOUR_WIDGET_KEY"></div>

<script type="module" crossorigin
        src="https://cdn.example.com/widget/widget.js"></script>

Replace https://cdn.example.com/widget/ with the URL the C-iCare team gave you, and YOUR_WIDGET_KEY with your channel key.

What must not change

  • id="livechat-app" — the widget looks for this element. Only one per page.
  • class="livechat-widget" — required. The widget's styles are written against #livechat-app.livechat-widget; without the class your layout will break.
  • data-widget-key — required. Without a valid key nothing renders at all.
  • type="module" on the script tag — required, the bundle is an ES module.

Optional: position and form fields

<div id="livechat-app"
     class="livechat-widget"
     data-widget-key="YOUR_WIDGET_KEY"
     data-pos="left"
     data-form="name,email,phone"></div>
  • data-pos — right (default) or left.
  • data-form — which fields the pre-chat form asks for. Any combination of name, email, phone. Left out, the form asks for Name + Email.

The form must ask for at least email or phone — a chat cannot start without one of those contacts.
